# Opportunity pre-screen scoring

Use the score to rank experiments, not businesses. Total = `A + B + C + D + E - F`; each positive category has 20 points and F is a 0–50 penalty.

| Category | Maximum | Score evidence, not rhetoric |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| A. Demand behavior | 20 | workaround, repetition, self-build, paid dissatisfaction, migration, independent evidence |
| B. Opportunity structure | 20 | narrow buyer, pain, clear value, suitable frequency, known alternative, wedge |
| C. Lifecycle hardness | 20 | comparison/replacement, quote/intent, trial/onboarding, usage/support/repeat |
| D. Experimentability | 20 | falsifiable claim, sellable offer, reachable users, low-cost channel, manual delivery, strong signal |
| E. Commercialization | 20 | price model fit, continuity, workflow/data embed, declining delivery cost, repeatable acquisition, expansion |
| F. Anti-pattern penalty | 0–50 | attention-only, no buyer/budget, unclear alternative, excess customization, declining margin, incumbent sufficiency, high switching cost, AI wrapper, hardware hype |

Score bands: 80+ immediate 3–7 day experiment; 65–79 customer discovery/quote test; 50–64 gather decisive desktop evidence; 35–49 watchlist; below 35 kill unless new evidence appears.

## Scoring discipline

- A missing field is `unknown`, not zero and not a guessed score. Report it as a confidence limitation.
- Apply penalties only where supporting evidence exists; do not manufacture negative evidence.
- Cap confidence at medium when the conclusion rests only on HN-derived signals or manually seeded labels.
