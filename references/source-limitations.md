# Source limitations

The bundled lifecycle and signal studies use Hacker News heavily. They are useful proxies for developer, startup, and technical discussion, but are not representative of every consumer, professional-service, offline, or regional market.

Product outcome labels and anchors include research seeds and manual classification. They are not audited financial outcomes. Term ambiguity can add noise. Attention and raw discussion volume are not demand or causality; normalized and downstream behavior is more useful but remains proxy evidence.

Therefore, use local corpus output to find and prioritize leads, then validate buyer, budget, delivery feasibility, and payment through explicit user-authorized research or experiments.
