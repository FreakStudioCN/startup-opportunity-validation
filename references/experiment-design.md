# 3–7 day experiment card

Write the core hypothesis in falsifiable form: `For [narrow buyer] facing [costly recurring problem], an offer of [specific outcome] at [price/commitment] will produce [strong signal] within [time cap].`

Include:

| Field | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Buyer and trigger | A narrow buyer plus the recent event that makes the problem urgent |
| Alternative | What they use now and its time, money, error, or risk cost |
| Minimum offer | A concrete result that can be manually delivered once |
| Reachable list | 30–100 targets, community members, or equivalent channel evidence |
| Strong signal | Meeting with data, quote request, paid pilot, deposit, or payment |
| Kill rule | A numerical or behavioral threshold set before launch |
| Modify rule | What changes if interest occurs but the strong signal does not |
| Economics log | Delivery time, software/AI cost, human review, hardware/BOM or compliance cost where applicable |

For hardware, add engineering risks: BOM, lead time, prototype feasibility, certification, reliability, returns, support, and warranty. Do not let a market signal clear those risks.
