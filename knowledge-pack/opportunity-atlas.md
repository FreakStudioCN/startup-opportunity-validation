# Opportunity atlas

This is a compact, evidence-linked view of the existing cross-signal opportunity clusters.

## C26 — Legal/document/compliance drafting workflow
- Score/stage: 85.2 / Worth 3-day experiment
- Buyer: legal/ops/compliance teams
- Problem: contracts, policies, reviews, and legal docs are document-heavy and slow
- Evidence: 6481 records; 14 signals; 5106 authors; 586 domains.
- Hypothesis: 我们观察到legal/ops/compliance teams正在因为contracts, policies, reviews, and legal docs are document-heavy and slow而采取搜索替代、自己造工具、workaround、付费或迁移动作。当前机会可能来自：LLMs reduce first draft and review cost, but liability remains。
- Next experiment: Sell manual contract review/extraction pilot; success is real docs + budget owner
